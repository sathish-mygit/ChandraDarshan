3. Information stored on your device

The App stores most information locally on your device using secure local storage (for example, Capacitor Preferences and similar mechanisms). We do not operate a backend server to receive your birth details or location.

3.1 App settings

  • Language preference (English, Hindi, Sanskrit, Telugu, or Tamil)
  • Lunar month system preference (Amanta or Purnimanta)
  • Location used for Panchang calculations — either a city you select or GPS coordinates if you grant permission

3.2 Optional birth profile (Jyotish)

If you choose to add a birth profile, the App may store locally:

  • an optional name
  • birth date
  • optional birth time (or “time unknown”)
  • birth place (city or coordinates)

This information is used only to calculate Jyotish-related features on your device. You can update or remove your birth profile at any time in Settings.

3.3 Cached calendar data

The App may cache computed Panchang results on your device to improve performance and support offline use.

3.4 Ad-related local settings

If ads are enabled, the App may store local ad-related settings and consent state on your device.

Data stored on your device remains on your device unless you uninstall the App, clear app data, or remove the information in Settings.

4. Location information

The App uses location only to calculate accurate Panchang times for your area.

  • If you select a city, the App uses that city’s coordinates and timezone.
  • If you choose GPS, the App requests location permission and reads your current coordinates and device timezone.
  • Location is used for calculations within the App. We do not upload your GPS coordinates or city selection to our servers.

In analytics, we may record only whether your location source is city or gps, not the actual coordinates or city name.

5. Information collected automatically

When analytics is enabled in a production build, we and our service providers may collect limited technical and usage information, such as:

  • app screens or pages viewed
  • time spent on screens
  • app interactions such as settings changes, glossary opens, and navigation between main sections
  • device or platform type (for example, native Android or web)
  • app language, month-system preference, whether a birth profile exists, and location source type
  • crash reports and error logs

5.1 What we do not send to analytics

We do not intentionally send the following to analytics:

  • birth date or birth time
  • birth name
  • precise latitude or longitude
  • city labels or other location identifiers

For birth profile actions, analytics may only indicate whether a birth time was provided, not the actual time or date.

5.2 When analytics is off

Development or non-production builds may have analytics disabled. In those builds, automatic collection described above may not occur.

Ad providers may collect information such as:

This collection is governed by Google’s and Meta’s policies and your device settings. Ads may be limited or blocked if required consent has not been given. We use controls such as frequency limits and engagement safeguards to reduce disruptive ad experiences.

For more information, see Google Ads technologies, the Google Privacy Policy, and the Meta Privacy Policy.
