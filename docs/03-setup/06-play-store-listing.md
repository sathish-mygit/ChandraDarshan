```
Chandra Darshan — see today's Moon phase, follow the Hindu Panchang, and explore personalized Jyotish from your date of birth. An Indian lunar calendar app for devotees, families, students, and anyone curious about the Moon, tithi, festivals, and traditional astrology.

🌙 MOON PHASE TODAY — CHANDRA DARSHAN ON YOUR PHONE
Wondering what the Moon looks like tonight? Chandra Darshan shows today's moon shape—waxing or waning, bright or slender—based on the current tithi. Perfect for Chandra Darshan observance, Amavasya and Purnima, Ekadashi fasting, and daily moon gazing. Watch the lunar cycle change night by night.

📅 HINDU PANCHANG & INDIAN LUNAR CALENDAR
Your daily Panchangam at a glance:
• Tithi today — which lunar day it is and when it changes
• Paksha — Shukla Paksha (waxing) or Krishna Paksha (waning)
• Maasa — Hindu month (Amanta or Purnimanta, your choice)
• Samvatsara — the 60-year Hindu year name
• Vara — weekday in the traditional calendar
• Nakshatra today — the Moon's star constellation
• Vikram Samvat & Shaka Samvat years
• Sunrise, sunset, moonrise & moonset for your city
• Abhijit Muhurta and auspicious daily timings
• Hindu festival reminders linked to the lunar calendar

Whether you call it Panchang, Panchangam, Hindu calendar, or Indian calendar—stay aligned with the Sanskrit lunar tradition, wherever you live.

🕉️ SANSKRIT CALENDAR IN YOUR LANGUAGE
Read Panchang in English, Hindi, Sanskrit, Telugu, or Tamil. See authentic terms—tithi, paksha, maasa, nakshatra—in Devanagari with clear explanations. A Sanskrit calendar experience with modern clarity. Ideal for Hindi Panchang, Telugu Panchangam, and Tamil calendar readers.

✨ PERSONALIZED JYOTISH & HOROSCOPE FROM BIRTH DATE
Add your date of birth, birth time (optional), and birth place for personal Jyotish—like a lightweight kundli and horoscope from your own data:

• Birth Moon sign (Rashi) and birth Nakshatra
• Daily Chandra Balam — is today's Moon favorable for you?
• Tarabala — your personal lunar strength today
• Vimshottari Dasha — Mahadasha, Antardasha & Pratyantardasha
• Sade Sati — Saturn's transit vs your Moon sign
• Birth chart (Kundli) with Lagna and planets in houses — when birth time is known
• Planet themes, yogas, and life milestone guidance
• Daily reading with beginner-friendly tooltips

Built-in glossary explains Rashi, Nakshatra, dasha, and more. Perfect for Jyotish beginners exploring Indian horoscope, birth chart, and Vedic astrology without overwhelm.

📍 ACCURATE FOR YOUR LOCATION — INDIA OR ABROAD
Set your city or use GPS so tithi, sunrise, moonrise, and Panchang timings match your location. Essential for NRIs and travelers who follow the Hindu lunar calendar and want correct local timings outside India.

🔒 OFFLINE PANCHANG — PRIVATE ON YOUR DEVICE
Panchang and Jyotish calculations run on your phone. No account. No login. Your birth details and horoscope data stay on your device. Open anytime—even without internet—for today's moon phase, tithi, and calendar.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 WHO IS IT FOR?
• Families following the Hindu calendar for festivals, vrats, and rituals
• Devotees observing Chandra Darshan, Ekadashi, Amavasya, and Purnima
• Students learning Sanskrit, Panchang, and Indian traditions
• Anyone searching moon phase today, tithi today, or nakshatra today
• People exploring Jyotish, kundli, rashi, nakshatra, and birth chart astrology
• Telugu, Tamil, Hindi, and Sanskrit readers who want Panchang in their language
• Users wanting horoscope insights from date of birth—not generic sun-sign predictions

🙏 HONEST & EDUCATIONAL
Chandra Darshan is a respectful guide to the Moon, Hindu Panchang, and personal Jyotish. Traditional calculations and gentle daily guidance—not fear-based predictions. For reflection, ritual, learning, and daily connection with Chandrama.

Download Chandra Darshan today. See the Moon. Know the tithi. Discover your Jyotish.
```

## Play Console notes

1. **First 2–3 lines** of the full description show before “Read more” — keep the hook and primary keywords there.
2. Add a **Hindi locale** (`hi-IN`) listing when ready — many searches are in Hindi.
3. Frame Jyotish as **educational guidance**, not fear-based predictions (Google Play astrology policy).
4. **~106 characters** of headroom remain in the full description for a support email or privacy line if needed.
5. Pair with screenshots and feature graphic — see [branding assets](05-branding-assets.md).
